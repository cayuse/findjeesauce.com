# Delivery links and affiliate income — study, 2026-10-27

Question (owner): link findjeesauce.com to Instacart / DoorDash / H-E-B online
"so people can get it, and side income". Is that a reasonable use? "It feels
a lot like exactly how this sort of thing is meant to work."

Owner, same day: "we don't need to tell them what store it's coming from,
just if they can get it from one of these locations."

Sources were read on 2026-10-27; program terms change, so re-check the
official pages before building on a number.

## Short answer

- **The links: yes, this is exactly how it's meant to work.** Brands sending
  shoppers to retailers is a whole product category ("where to buy":
  PriceSpider, Destini, MikMak), and Instacart gives brands a free page made
  for it (below). It fits the owner's rule: Instacart picks the retailer from
  the shopper's address, and we only need to say "you can get it delivered".
- **The side income: mostly no, not as a brand.** Affiliate programs pay a
  third party for sending customers. A brand promoting its own product is on
  the PAYING side of that market (ads), and Instacart says so in its own
  terms. Treat delivery links as a way to sell more sauce, not as a revenue
  line.

## What each option actually offers

### Instacart: brand page (the fit)
- **Brand pages**: "a curated, shoppable destination where the consumer can
  explore the brand's product portfolio from their favorite retailers";
  brands get "unique URLs" for their own off-platform marketing. Reported
  free to create. Instacart chooses the retailer by the shopper's location,
  which is what the owner wants.
- Ask: does a JeeSauce brand page exist or can we create one, through an
  Instacart Ads account. That's the next step, not code.
- Sources: https://instacart.com/company/ads/off-platform-solutions ;
  https://www.modernretail.co/technology/instacart-is-betting-on-youtube-to-turbocharge-its-871m-ad-business/

### Instacart: affiliate program (not for us)
- Runs on Impact. Up to $10 per new customer (publishers), 10% (influencers),
  5% of the cart for Developer Platform apps.
- **Brands are not eligible.** Their FAQ, on showcasing CPG brands: "Unfortunately
  no", unless the Instacart team says otherwise; brands are sent to Instacart Ads.
- Developer Platform (IDP): "primarily designed for B2C integrations" (meal
  planning, cooking apps, publishers); **applications closed, no waitlist**;
  and "directing users to a specific merchant is not supported".
- Sources: https://company.instacart.com/affiliate ;
  https://company.instacart.com/business/developers ;
  https://docs.instacart.com/developer_platform_api/faq

### DoorDash
- An affiliate program exists on Impact, but the terms we found are from
  third-party directories that disagree (from ~$3 to $50 per new customer,
  different cookie windows), and it pays for NEW DoorDash customers, not for
  selling our sauce. No official terms were read. Same caution as Instacart
  about brands; ask before assuming eligibility.
- Sources: https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/doordash-consumer/ ;
  https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/doordash/

### H-E-B (heb.com)
- No heb.com affiliate program found (only a defunct H-E-B México listing).
- A plain link to our product on heb.com still works and costs nothing: one
  URL per flavor covers every H-E-B store, which is most of our stores.
- H-E-B also sells through Instacart in Texas (since 2015; current coverage
  unverified), so the Instacart brand page may cover H-E-B too.
- Source: https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/heb-mx-affiliate-program/ ;
  https://www.andnowuknow.com/headlines/h-e-b-partners-instacart-grocery-delivery/jessica-donnel/46812

## Recommendation

1. **H-E-B product links first** (free, ours to make, most stores).
2. **Instacart brand page** next: one link, "Get it delivered", Instacart
   picks the store. Needs an Instacart Ads conversation, not code.
3. **Skip affiliate income** unless an Instacart or DoorDash rep confirms a
   brand may earn on its own products; if one does, the disclosure line and
   "the fee never chooses the order" rules in FLAVOR_FINDER.md apply.
4. Where-to-buy vendors (PriceSpider, Destini, MikMak) do all of this as a
   paid service. findjeesauce.com already does the store half; we only need
   the delivery half, so building it ourselves stays reasonable.
